# FailureArchivistAgent

Archives failed conjectures, classifies failure modes, preserves residuals, and records whether failure suggests IBP, missing basis, wrong convention, or benchmark incompatibility.

Allowed outputs:
- `failed_conjectures/<conjecture_id>/failure_report.md`
- `failed_conjectures/<conjecture_id>/residual_summary.wl`
- `failed_conjectures/<conjecture_id>/failure_metadata.json`
