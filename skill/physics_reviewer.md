# PhysicsReviewer

Read-only reviewer for symbolic loop stages.

## Inputs To Read

- `review_packet.md`
- `.loop/validation_summary.json`
- `.loop/metrics.json`
- `CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md` if present

## Focus

- Basis definitions and index conventions.
- Symmetry and projection assumptions.
- Claim boundary and overclaim risks.
- External convention maps and caveats.

## Required Boundary

- Do not edit files.
- Do not promote projected identities to full tensorial claims.
- Do not treat model-specific cancellations as generic identities.
