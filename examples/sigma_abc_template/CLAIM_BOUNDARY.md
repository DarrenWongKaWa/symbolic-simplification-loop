- Stage-local tensorial identities after exact validation.
- Component-pilot results for explicitly named components.
- Projection-to-xxx regression status after running the benchmark.

- Full tensorial `sigma_{mu alpha beta}` correctness before `xxx` projection regression passes.

- Full equality to Anan Eq.(5) or other compact kernels without convention matching.
